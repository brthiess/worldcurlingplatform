# World Curling Platform

Shared language for the people, competitions, and records of the World Curling Platform. Definitions begin with the user's stated workflows and are refined during the design interview.

## Language

**World Curling Platform**:
The proposed standalone system for World Curling's curling registrations, competitions, rankings, and public information.
_Avoid_: CurlHub as a name for this project.

**World Curling administrator**:
A platform administrator with authority to determine which events are allowed in the system, including events that award no ranking points.

**Event approver**:
A person authorized by World Curling, directly or through an explicitly permitted delegation, to approve event applications within an assigned scope. An event approver may belong to another organization, such as Curling Canada.
_Avoid_: World Curling administrator when referring to every person who can approve an event.

**Player profile**:
The record describing a curling player, which can exist before that player creates an account. A World Curling-approved account link permits the player to maintain biography and handedness and submit a photo for approval.
_Avoid_: Account as a synonym for the player's sport record.

**Player-profile claim**:
A request to link an existing player record to a registered account. World Curling administrator approval establishes the link; creating an account alone does not.

**Fan**:
A public audience member following curling events, teams, players, scores, standings, and rankings.

**Event organizer**:
A person responsible for registering and administering a curling event.

**Event series**:
A recurring named competition, such as the Prairie Open, across its dated editions.
_Avoid_: Event when specifically referring to the recurring series.

**Event edition**:
One dated occurrence of an event series, with its organizer, dates, and venues. Next year's occurrence is a new edition.
_Avoid_: Reusing an earlier edition for a later year's competition.

**Division**:
A separately run competition within an event edition, with its own entries, format, games, and results. An edition with only one competition still has one division.
_Avoid_: Using discipline or eligibility category as a synonym for division.

**Event application**:
A request for an event edition to be admitted to the platform. Approval permits its public listing; opening entry registration and establishing division ranking eligibility are separate decisions.
_Avoid_: Team registration when referring to an organizer's event application.

**Event entry**:
A team's participation record in one division, submitted by the team or entered manually by an organizer. Organizers may select an existing team, assemble a lineup from existing players, or add players who do not yet exist.
_Avoid_: Event application when referring to a team's entry.

**Event lineup**:
The players recorded for one event entry, which may differ from a registered team's season roster through temporary substitutions. An incomplete or unresolved lineup may remain provisional.
_Avoid_: Season roster when referring only to the players entered for one event.

**Core roster**:
The regular players whose permanent membership defines a team, excluding designated alternates and coaches. Core membership is distinct from who happens to play in an individual event or game.

**Team**:
A competitive identity defined by its core roster and discipline. A permanent core-player addition or departure creates a different team; alternate, coach, name, or position changes do not, and an identical core roster can recur across seasons.
_Avoid_: An enduring identity that automatically survives permanent player changes.

**Season roster**:
A registered team's core and alternate players for a season, separate from an individual event lineup. Core membership changes create a different team, while alternate changes preserve the team identity.

**Provisional player**:
A player record created while its relationship to possible existing player records is unresolved. It can be used for an event entry while authorized administrators review the possible duplicate.

**Scorekeeper**:
A person assigned to record game scores for an event, including a volunteer. The assigned scorekeeper may confirm and correct the final result without administrator approval; changes remain auditable.
_Avoid_: Volunteer as a synonym for every scoring or administrative role.

**Game lineup**:
The lineup recorded for an entry in a particular game, defaulting to its event lineup. The scorekeeper records differences when applicable; a defaulted lineup is distinct from independently verified participation.
_Avoid_: Changing the event lineup to describe a substitution limited to one game.

**Competition draw**:
The arrangement of games and advancement paths within a division, prepared from its chosen competition format.
_Avoid_: Draw session when referring to the complete competition structure.

**Draw session**:
A scheduled time block for games on the available ice sheets.
_Avoid_: Competition draw when referring only to a scheduled round of play.

**Confirmed game result**:
A final game result confirmed by an assigned scorekeeper, used to advance the competition draw. Confirmation records the responsible person and time; the scorekeeper may subsequently correct it with an audit history.
_Avoid_: Live score when referring to a confirmed result.

**Division finalization**:
The organizer's recording of a division's completed results as a dated result snapshot. Later score corrections remain possible and can lead to a revised finalization.
_Avoid_: Ranking approval as a synonym for sporting result finalization.

**Ranking publication**:
A World Curling-approved release of a WCTRS ranking list for an effective date. A corrected publication preserves earlier approved versions and their history.
_Avoid_: Live projected rankings when referring to approved rankings.

**Line score**:
The sequence of points scored by each side in individual ends of a curling game.
_Avoid_: Final score when referring to the individual end scores.
