## Implemented policy / outstanding verification

Current edition organizers and global administrators may freeze normal charges, revise future fees, record partial discounts, full remaining waivers and staff-attested external receipts, always with reasons and reviewed financial versions. Payment representatives may read only their current exact team-season entries and cannot attest staff receipts. A charge freezes on the first admission request/invitation or explicit staff pricing; fee revisions append a new amount/terms version for future charges while currency stays fixed and opened sporting/capacity terms remain immutable. Legacy admissions can use only matching retained original terms; unknown terms are not evidence of zero or payment. Credits cannot exceed due. External references are unique per division and remain distinguishable from provider transactions. No checkout/refund engine is introduced.

Edition-exclusive locking serializes credits and capacity; time and authority are checked after waits and before exact receipt replay. Clearing a live hold accepts it without creating another place; clearing after effective expiry retains the released capacity and requires staff attention. Financial balance, admission, lineup, ranking and future withdrawal/refund state remain separate.
