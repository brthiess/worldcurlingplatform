# Own operational records in a standalone World Curling Platform

The proposed platform will own its registration, identity, competition, and ranking records without depending on CurlHub or another existing operational system. The user chose this boundary so the offer to World Curling can demonstrate a complete platform rather than require an existing administrative product. The crawler supplies an initial historical dataset; its models, publication rules, and ongoing operation do not define this platform's authority or runtime behavior. Partner systems may submit data through the platform's authorized APIs, as required in the original request.

This records the user's design direction for the offer, not World Curling endorsement. Later Q57-Q59 accept explicitly scoped partner scoring/confirmation and administrator-reviewed, provenance-preserving historical-import batches. The local parent spec captures those refinements and the explicit crawler source.
